## Why this question gets confusing answers

Two products with similar names sit in very different commercial positions in Australia, and
a great deal of published material treats them as interchangeable.

Dragon Medical One is the established front-end medical dictation product. It has been sold in
Australia for years through authorised resellers, it has a local support ecosystem, and Microsoft
maintains Australian and New Zealand specific enablement material for it.

Dragon Copilot is the newer unified product that brings ambient documentation and generative AI
together with dictation. It is coming to the Australian market, but Microsoft has not yet announced an
Australian release date.

So a clinician in Brisbane asking "can I buy this" gets a straightforward yes for one product and
a considerably more complicated answer for the other.

## What Microsoft publishes for Australia and New Zealand

Microsoft runs a Dragon Medical One learning series directed at Australia and New Zealand. It
covers getting started, voice training to improve recognition accuracy, templates, and creating
step-by-step commands to automate EMR navigation. There is also a course on Dragon Medical One
analytics in Nuance Management Center.

The detail worth noticing is what is absent. Dragon Copilot is not mentioned anywhere in that
Australian and New Zealand material. That is consistent with the availability position rather
than an oversight.

## Buying it in Australia

Dragon Medical One is not a shelf product. It is sold through authorised Australian resellers who
handle licensing, the clinical system configuration, dictation hardware and clinician onboarding
together, because in practice those things fail or succeed as one piece of work. Clinicians and
practice managers who want to [buy Dragon Medical One in Australia](https://www.voicerecognition.com.au/pages/dragon-medical-one)
can do so today, which remains the sharpest practical difference between the two products in this
market.

If you are weighing the two up rather than checking availability,
